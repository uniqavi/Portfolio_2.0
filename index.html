Research

Smart Home Access Control

Mixed-Methods HCI Study

A five day WhatsApp diary study across three households found that whoever sets up the smart speaker quietly becomes the household's admin, with real power over lights, thermostats, and even surveillance. The fix isn't better AI, it's better permissions: tiered access, family dashboards, and presence aware commands borrowed straight from software design.

SHAC

Racing the Clock

Physiological Computing Research

We put 32 people through the same math problems twice, once against a ticking clock, once without, and watched heart rates spike by more than 20 bpm and error rates jump tenfold under pressure. Turns out a countdown timer isn't a neutral UI element, it's a stress test, which has real implications for how we design quizzes and checkout flows.
